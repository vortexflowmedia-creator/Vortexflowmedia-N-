Privacy Policy

How VortexFlow Media collects, uses, and protects your information.

INDIA • GDPR AWARE

VortexFlow Media ("we", "us") operates https://vortexflowmedia.vercel.app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our website or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the site or submitting the contact form, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what you voluntarily provide and what is technically necessary to run the site:

  • Contact form: name, email, phone (optional), project details, and any message you submit. Submitted via api/content and/or EmailJS to vortexflowmedia@gmail.com and stored in Supabase (bucelcptyosulatkkpuw.supabase.co) for follow-up.
  • Technical data: anonymized IP, device/browser type, and pages visited — collected via Vercel hosting logs. No custom analytics cookies are set by us.
  • WhatsApp: if you click the WhatsApp float or wa.me link, your WhatsApp client shares data with WhatsApp per their policy; we do not control it.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to inquiries, provide quotes, and deliver requested creative services.
  • To maintain and secure the website, prevent abuse, and comply with law.
  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. Cookies

We do not set non-essential cookies. Essential cookies may be set by Vercel for security/load balancing and by your browser for session handling. Fonts are loaded from fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com which may set cookies per Google's policy. You can block cookies in your browser settings without breaking core browsing.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Vercel — hosting & edge delivery (privacy).
  • Supabase — contact storage (privacy).
  • EmailJS (api.emailjs.com) — if enabled for contact emails.
  • Google Fonts — typography delivery.
  • WhatsApp — wa.me link handling.

Each provider processes data under its own policy and, where applicable, as a processor on our instructions.

5. Data Retention

Contact submissions are retained as long as necessary to handle your inquiry and for legitimate business records, then deleted or anonymized upon request. Server logs are retained per Vercel/Supabase retention windows.

6. Your Rights

Under applicable Indian law you may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data, and you may object to processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at vortexflowmedia@gmail.com or srivastavsaiyeluri@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe and may need to verify your identity.

7. Security

We use HTTPS (HSTS), CSP headers, and access-controlled Supabase storage as configured in supabase-schema.sql and vercel.json. No system is 100% secure, but we apply reasonable measures and will notify you if a breach materially affects your data where required by law.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us for deletion.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect service or legal changes. The "Updated" date above will be revised and material changes will be highlighted on this page. Continued use after an update constitutes acceptance.
